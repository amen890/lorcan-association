EDSA

Ethiopian Dental Students' Association

🎯 Goal

To unite dental students across Ethiopia and promote excellence in dental education, research, and community service.

🚀 Mission

Developing competent dental professionals through education, skill-building, and oral health advocacy programs.

👁️ Vision

A future where every Ethiopian has access to quality dental care delivered by well-trained professionals.

📅 Programs & Events

Oral Health Day Celebration

Free dental screenings and awareness in communities.

Dental Skills Workshop

Hands-on training sessions on modern dental techniques.

School Oral Health Program

Educational visits to schools promoting dental hygiene.

National Dental Convention

Annual gathering featuring lectures and competitions.
